Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Mosaic Life is a personal life operating system designed for use by you and your family. This policy explains what data is collected, how it is stored, and your rights.

1. Who we are

Mosaic Life is operated by Jagdeep Singh Virdi for personal and family use. Contact: jagdeep.singh.virdi@gmail.com

2. Data we collect

  • Account data — name, email address, password hash (managed by Clerk).
  • Health data — medications, prescriptions, blood test results, cycle tracking, appointments. Stored in your private account only.
  • Financial data — subscriptions, loans, credit cards, bills. Stored in your private account only.
  • Calendar & tasks — events, tasks, learning progress, habits.
  • Family data — family directory, shared shopping lists, household chores.
  • Uploaded files — prescription PDFs and images sent to AI for extraction. Files are not stored permanently after extraction.

3. How data is stored

All data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted on Neon.tech (or locally during development). Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Authentication is handled by Clerk, which is SOC 2 Type II certified.

4. AI processing

When you use the "Extract with AI" feature, the content of uploaded documents (prescriptions, blood reports) is sent to Google Gemini or a locally-hosted Ollama model for structured data extraction. No document content is retained by Mosaic Life after extraction. Google Gemini is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Third-party services

  • Clerk — authentication and user management.
  • Google Gemini — AI document extraction.
  • Resend — transactional email (household invites).
  • TheMealDB — recipe data (no personal data sent).
  • Nager.Date / public sports APIs — holiday and sports data (no personal data sent).

6. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Data is only shared with the service providers listed above, solely to provide the app's functionality.

7. Household sharing

If you accept a household invitation, certain data (shopping list, chores, family directory, family moments) becomes visible to all members of your household. Personal health, finance, and learning data remains private to your individual account.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, contact jagdeep.singh.virdi@gmail.com. You can also export your data from Settings → Export.

9. Data retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, all personal data will be permanently removed within 30 days.

10. Applicable law

This app is operated from Thailand. It is intended to comply with Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where applicable.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes.
